### 一、函数的和、差、积、商的求导法则

定理2.2.1 如果函数 $u(x), \nu(x)$ 都在点 $x$ 处可导，那么

（1）函数 $y = u(x)\pm \nu (x)$ 在点 $x$ 处可导，且

$$
[ u (x) \pm v (x) ] ^ {\prime} = u ^ {\prime} (x) \pm v ^ {\prime} (x),
$$

即

$$
(u \pm v) ^ {\prime} = u ^ {\prime} \pm v ^ {\prime}.
$$

（2）函数 $y = u(x) \cdot \nu(x)$ 在点 $x$ 处可导，且

$$
[ u (x) \cdot v (x) ] ^ {\prime} = u ^ {\prime} (x) \cdot v (x) + u (x) \cdot v ^ {\prime} (x),
$$

即

$$
(u v) ^ {\prime} = u ^ {\prime} v + u v ^ {\prime}.
$$

特别地， $(ku)' = ku'$ （ $k$ 为常数）.

（3）当 $v(x)\neq 0$ 时，函数 $\frac{u(x)}{v(x)}$ 在点 $x$ 处可导，且

$$
\left[ \frac {u (x)}{v (x)} \right] ^ {\prime} = \frac {u ^ {\prime} (x) v (x) - u (x) v ^ {\prime} (x)}{v ^ {2} (x)},
$$

即

$$
\left(\frac {u}{v}\right) ^ {\prime} = \frac {u ^ {\prime} v - u v ^ {\prime}}{v ^ {2}}.
$$

特别地， $\left(\frac{1}{v}\right)' = -\frac{v'}{v^2}.$ 

证 给 $x$ 以增量 $\Delta x$ ，相应地，函数 $u(x)$ 与 $\nu(x)$ 各有增量 $\Delta u$ 与 $\Delta \nu$ ，即

$$
\Delta u = u (x + \Delta x) - u (x), \quad \Delta v = v (x + \Delta x) - v (x).
$$

（1）函数 $y = u(x)\pm \nu (x)$ 有增量

$$
\begin{array}{l} \Delta y = [ u (x + \Delta x) \pm v (x + \Delta x) ] - [ u (x) \pm v (x) ] \\ = [ (u (x) + \Delta u) \pm (\nu (x) + \Delta \nu) ] - [ u (x) \pm \nu (x) ] \\ = \Delta u \pm \Delta v, \\ \end{array}
$$

则

$$
\frac {\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \frac {\Delta u}{\Delta x} \pm \frac {\Delta v}{\Delta x}.
$$

由于 $u(x)$ 与 $\nu (x)$ 都在点 $x$ 处可导，即

$$
\lim  _ {\Delta x \rightarrow 0} \frac {\Delta u}{\Delta x} = u ^ {\prime} (x), \quad \lim  _ {\Delta x \rightarrow 0} \frac {\Delta v}{\Delta x} = v ^ {\prime} (x),
$$

于是

$$
\lim  _ {\Delta x \to 0} \frac {\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \lim  _ {\Delta x \to 0} \frac {\Delta u}{\Delta x} \pm \lim  _ {\Delta x \to 0} \frac {\Delta v}{\Delta x} = u ^ {\prime} (x) \pm v ^ {\prime} (x).
$$

这表示 $y = u(x) \pm v(x)$ 在点 $x$ 处可导，且

$$
(u \pm v) ^ {\prime} = u ^ {\prime} \pm v ^ {\prime}.
$$

（2）函数 $y = u(x)\cdot \nu (x)$ 有增量

$$
\begin{array}{l} \Delta y = u (x + \Delta x) \cdot v (x + \Delta x) - u (x) \cdot v (x) \\ = [ u (x) + \Delta u ] \cdot [ v (x) + \Delta v ] - u (x) \cdot v (x) \\ = v (x) \cdot \Delta u + u (x) \cdot \Delta v + \Delta u \cdot \Delta v, \\ \end{array}
$$

则

$$
\frac {\Delta y}{\Delta x} = v (x) \cdot \frac {\Delta u}{\Delta x} + u (x) \cdot \frac {\Delta v}{\Delta x} + \frac {\Delta u}{\Delta x} \cdot \Delta v,
$$

因为函数 $\nu (x)$ 在点 $x$ 处可导，所以 $\nu (x)$ 在点 $x$ 处连续，于是当 $\Delta x\to 0$ 时， $\Delta \nu \rightarrow 0$ .又当 $\Delta x\to 0$ 时，

$$
\frac {\Delta u}{\Delta x} \rightarrow u ^ {\prime} (x), \frac {\Delta v}{\Delta x} \rightarrow v ^ {\prime} (x) ,
$$

所以

$$
\begin{array}{l} \lim  _ {\Delta x \rightarrow 0} \frac {\Delta y}{\Delta x} = v (x) \cdot \lim  _ {\Delta x \rightarrow 0} \frac {\Delta u}{\Delta x} + u (x) \cdot \lim  _ {\Delta x \rightarrow 0} \frac {\Delta v}{\Delta x} + \lim  _ {\Delta x \rightarrow 0} \frac {\Delta u}{\Delta x} \cdot \lim  _ {\Delta x \rightarrow 0} \Delta v \\ = v (x) \cdot u ^ {\prime} (x) + u (x) \cdot v ^ {\prime} (x), \\ \end{array}
$$

即 $y = u(x)\cdot v(x)$ 在点 $x$ 处可导，且

$$
(u v) ^ {\prime} = u ^ {\prime} v + u v ^ {\prime}.
$$

（3）函数 $y = \frac{u(x)}{v(x)} (v(x)\neq 0)$ 有增量

$$
\begin{array}{l} \Delta y = \frac {u (x + \Delta x)}{v (x + \Delta x)} - \frac {u (x)}{v (x)} = \frac {u (x) + \Delta u}{v (x) + \Delta v} - \frac {u (x)}{v (x)} \\ = \frac {\Delta u \cdot v (x) - \Delta v \cdot u (x)}{[ v (x) + \Delta v ] \cdot v (x)}, \\ \end{array}
$$

则

$$
\frac {\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \frac {1}{[ v (x) + \Delta v ] \cdot v (x)} \left[ \frac {\Delta u}{\Delta x} \cdot v (x) - \frac {\Delta v}{\Delta x} \cdot u (x) \right].
$$

又由于当 $\Delta x\to 0$ 时，

$$
\Delta v \rightarrow 0, \frac {\Delta u}{\Delta x} \rightarrow u ^ {\prime} (x), \frac {\Delta v}{\Delta x} \rightarrow v ^ {\prime} (x),
$$

所以

$$
\lim  _ {\Delta x \rightarrow 0} \frac {\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \frac {u ^ {\prime} (x) \cdot v (x) - u (x) \cdot v ^ {\prime} (x)}{v ^ {2} (x)},
$$

即 $y = \frac{u(x)}{v(x)} (v(x)\neq 0)$ 在点 $x$ 处可导，且

$$
\left[ \frac {u (x)}{v (x)} \right] ^ {\prime} = \frac {u ^ {\prime} (x) \cdot v (x) - u (x) \cdot v ^ {\prime} (x)}{v ^ {2} (x)}.
$$

推论2.2.1 若有限个函数 $u_{1}(x), u_{2}(x), \dots, u_{n}(x)$ 均在点 $x$ 处可导，则

$$
\begin{array}{l} \left(u _ {1} \pm u _ {2} \pm u _ {3} \pm \dots \pm u _ {n}\right) ^ {\prime} = u _ {1} ^ {\prime} \pm u _ {2} ^ {\prime} \pm u _ {3} ^ {\prime} \pm \dots \pm u _ {n} ^ {\prime}, \\ \left(u _ {1} u _ {2} u _ {3} \dots u _ {n}\right) ^ {\prime} = u _ {1} ^ {\prime} u _ {2} u _ {3} \dots u _ {n} + u _ {1} u _ {2} ^ {\prime} u _ {3} \dots u _ {n} + \dots + u _ {1} u _ {2} u _ {3} \dots u _ {n} ^ {\prime}. \\ \end{array}
$$

例2.2.1 设 $y = \frac{1}{4} x^4 + \sqrt{x} + \cos x - \ln 3$ ，求 $y'$ 和 $y''|_{x = \frac{\pi}{2}}$ .

解 $y^\prime = \left(\frac{1}{4} x^4 +\sqrt{x} +\cos x - \ln 3\right)^{\prime}$ 

$$
\begin{array}{l} = \left(\frac {1}{4} x ^ {4}\right) ^ {\prime} + (\sqrt {x}) ^ {\prime} + (\cos x) ^ {\prime} + (- \ln 3) ^ {\prime} \\ = x ^ {3} + \frac {1}{2 \sqrt {x}} - \sin x. \\ \left. y ^ {\prime} \right| _ {x = \frac {\pi}{2}} = \left(\frac {\pi}{2}\right) ^ {3} + \frac {1}{2 \sqrt {\frac {\pi}{2}}} - \sin \frac {\pi}{2} = \frac {\pi^ {3}}{8} + \frac {1}{\sqrt {2 \pi}} - 1. \\ \end{array}
$$

例2.2.2 设 $y = 3x^{2}\sin x$ ，求 $y^\prime$ 

解 $y^\prime = (3x^2\sin x)^\prime = 3(x^2\sin x)^\prime$ 

$$
= 3 \left[ \left(x ^ {2}\right) ^ {\prime} \sin x + x ^ {2} (\sin x) ^ {\prime} \right]
$$

$$
= 3 (2 x \sin x + x ^ {2} \cos x).
$$

例2.2.3 设 $y = x^{2}\mathrm{e}^{x}\ln x$ ，求 $y^\prime$ 

解 $y^\prime = (x^2\mathrm{e}^x\ln x)^\prime$ 

$$
\begin{array}{l} = \left(x ^ {2}\right) ^ {\prime} \cdot e ^ {x} \ln x + x ^ {2} \cdot \left(e ^ {x}\right) ^ {\prime} \cdot \ln x + x ^ {2} e ^ {x} \cdot \left(\ln x\right) ^ {\prime} \\ = 2 x \mathrm {e} ^ {x} \ln x + x ^ {2} \mathrm {e} ^ {x} \ln x + x \mathrm {e} ^ {x} \\ = x \mathrm {e} ^ {x} (1 + 2 \ln x + x \ln x). \\ \end{array}
$$

例2.2.4 设 $y = \tan x$ ，求 $y^\prime$ 

解 $y = \tan x = \frac{\sin x}{\cos x},$ 

$$
\begin{array}{l} y ^ {\prime} = (\tan x) ^ {\prime} = \left(\frac {\sin x}{\cos x}\right) ^ {\prime} \\ = \frac {(\sin x) ^ {\prime} \cdot \cos x - (\sin x) \cdot (\cos x) ^ {\prime}}{\cos^ {2} x} \\ = \frac {\cos^ {2} x + \sin^ {2} x}{\cos^ {2} x} = \sec^ {2} x. \\ \end{array}
$$

由例2.2.4有

$$
(\tan x) ^ {\prime} = \sec^ {2} x \left(x \neq k \pi + \frac {\pi}{2}, k = 0, \pm 1, \pm 2, \dots\right).
$$

同理可得

$$
\begin{array}{l} \left(\cot x\right) ^ {\prime} = - \csc^ {2} x (x \neq k \pi , k = 0, \pm 1, \pm 2, \dots); \\ (\sec x) ^ {\prime} = \sec x \cdot \tan x \left(x \neq k \pi + \frac {\pi}{2}, k = 0, \pm 1, \pm 2, \dots\right); \\ (\csc x) ^ {\prime} = - \csc x \cdot \cot x (x \neq k \pi , k = 0, \pm 1, \pm 2, \dots). \\ \end{array}
$$

例2.2.5 设 $y = \frac{x^2 - 1}{x^2 + 1}$ ，求 $y'$ 

解 $y^\prime = \left(\frac{x^2 - 1}{x^2 + 1}\right)$ 

$$
\begin{array}{l} = \frac {\left(x ^ {2} - 1\right) ^ {\prime} \cdot \left(x ^ {2} + 1\right) - \left(x ^ {2} - 1\right) \cdot \left(x ^ {2} + 1\right) ^ {\prime}}{\left(x ^ {2} + 1\right) ^ {2}} \\ = \frac {4 x}{\left(x ^ {2} + 1\right) ^ {2}}. \\ \end{array}
$$

例2.2.6 设 $y = \log_a x (a > 0, a \neq 1, x > 0)$ ，求 $y'$ .

解 $y^\prime = (\log_ax)' = \left(\frac{\ln x}{\ln a}\right)' = \frac{1}{x\ln a}.$ 

由例2.2.6有

$$
\left(\log_ {a} x\right) ^ {\prime} = \frac {1}{x \ln a} (a > 0, a \neq 1), x \in (0, + \infty).
$$
