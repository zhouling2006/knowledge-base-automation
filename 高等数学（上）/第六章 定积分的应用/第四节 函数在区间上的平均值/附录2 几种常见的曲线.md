### 附录2 几种常见的曲线

![image](https://cdn-mineru.openxlab.org.cn/result/2026-04-03/ccf3acff-bbac-48d8-97dd-e31ada0e3759/dad3fbf08680565d920d5f122812f02d2646f6f531b64e58c210bf84f33f721f.jpg)


（1）三次抛物线

$$
y = a x ^ {3}
$$

![image](https://cdn-mineru.openxlab.org.cn/result/2026-04-03/ccf3acff-bbac-48d8-97dd-e31ada0e3759/41524be965db12e4f5249cd75304fc3d1fcd0c04477f5b73a76a02dbea341459.jpg)


（2）半立方抛物线

$$
y ^ {2} = a x ^ {3}
$$

![image](https://cdn-mineru.openxlab.org.cn/result/2026-04-03/ccf3acff-bbac-48d8-97dd-e31ada0e3759/6fbc3e1c4887bbe6beff039525032eed0301821a7af6da7f3cdcc79e351d5f37.jpg)


（3）概率密度曲线

$$
y = \mathrm {e} ^ {- x ^ {2}}
$$

![image](https://cdn-mineru.openxlab.org.cn/result/2026-04-03/ccf3acff-bbac-48d8-97dd-e31ada0e3759/75b0bf0102303b4332f9996d6d465ef1c0c725c5b99d4844e687c8c315b96e47.jpg)


（4）笛卡儿叶形线

$$
x ^ {3} + y ^ {3} - 3 a x y = 0, a > 0
$$

$$
x = \frac {3 a t}{1 + t ^ {3}}, y = \frac {3 a t ^ {2}}{1 + t ^ {3}}
$$

![image](https://cdn-mineru.openxlab.org.cn/result/2026-04-03/ccf3acff-bbac-48d8-97dd-e31ada0e3759/c972bc898e5d5abedc112f5d295d8bc0dfe8c9747422d53ec35cd59c0f778fb3.jpg)


（5）心形线（外摆线的一种）

$$
x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} + a x = a \sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}},
$$

$$
\rho = a (1 - \cos \varphi)
$$

![image](https://cdn-mineru.openxlab.org.cn/result/2026-04-03/ccf3acff-bbac-48d8-97dd-e31ada0e3759/d84d770398c6876e8ae04dc8e826016ce18f9753e4b821c2f3b53ea2c9f6e294.jpg)


（6）阿基米德螺线

$$
\rho = a \varphi
$$

![image](https://cdn-mineru.openxlab.org.cn/result/2026-04-03/ccf3acff-bbac-48d8-97dd-e31ada0e3759/b0cfd906c4884b9f0bc3c17a5c5e2dd4f04647ceb44d9c124579829e9c9415fc.jpg)


（7）伯努利双纽线

$$
\left(x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}\right) ^ {2} = a ^ {2} \left(x ^ {2} - y ^ {2}\right),
$$

$$
\rho^ {2} = a ^ {2} \cos 2 \varphi
$$

![image](https://cdn-mineru.openxlab.org.cn/result/2026-04-03/ccf3acff-bbac-48d8-97dd-e31ada0e3759/5e43b7343e827fcc144dd54339ef655c320492232264d84d40b552305974f0bc.jpg)


（9）星形线（内摆线的一种）

$$
x ^ {\frac {2}{3}} + y ^ {\frac {2}{3}} = a ^ {\frac {2}{3}},
$$

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = a \cos^ {3} \theta , \\ y = a \sin^ {3} \theta \end{array} \right.
$$

![image](https://cdn-mineru.openxlab.org.cn/result/2026-04-03/ccf3acff-bbac-48d8-97dd-e31ada0e3759/b94d3e7b49e6519abf4027a9d29a48fc07a4c316d53f3a49e64924b2b0487324.jpg)


（11）对数螺线

$$
\rho = \mathrm {e} ^ {a \varphi}
$$

![image](https://cdn-mineru.openxlab.org.cn/result/2026-04-03/ccf3acff-bbac-48d8-97dd-e31ada0e3759/c3da8050fae04c67ca87fe4e1c015a98e7733f4c764e2404d0daa0d64fc17e26.jpg)


（8）伯努利双纽线

$$
\left(x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}\right) ^ {2} = 2 a ^ {2} x y,
$$

$$
\rho^ {2} = a ^ {2} \sin 2 \varphi
$$

![image](https://cdn-mineru.openxlab.org.cn/result/2026-04-03/ccf3acff-bbac-48d8-97dd-e31ada0e3759/8c80db17a398fea8bedd092fcba0b4bb689b039707acf81bfb1bdb1286d4ced1.jpg)


（10）摆线

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = a (\theta - \sin \theta), \\ y = a (1 - \cos \theta) \end{array} \right.
$$

![image](https://cdn-mineru.openxlab.org.cn/result/2026-04-03/ccf3acff-bbac-48d8-97dd-e31ada0e3759/7e16cfb9f9ce71520a4e47e9ca42c713e637657d5e7c114aa8c86e07d5168b7b.jpg)


（12）三叶玫瑰线

$$
\rho = a \sin 3 \varphi
$$
