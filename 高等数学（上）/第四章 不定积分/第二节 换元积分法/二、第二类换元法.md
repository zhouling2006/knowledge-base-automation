### 二、第二类换元法

第一类换元法是把一个比较复杂的不定积分 $\int g(x)\mathrm{d}x$ 化成下列形式

$$
\int g (x) \mathrm {d} x = \int f [ \varphi (x) ] \varphi^ {\prime} (x) \mathrm {d} x = \int f [ \varphi (x) ] \mathrm {d} [ \varphi (x) ] = \int f (u) \mathrm {d} u | _ {u = \varphi (x)},
$$

然后通过不定积分的线性运算及基本积分公式或已知结论得出结果. 但有时遇到的是相反的情形, 即不定积分 $\int f(x) \mathrm{d}x$ 较难求. 这时, 作变量代换 $x = \varphi(t)$ , 有

$$
\int f (x) \mathrm {d} x = \int f [ \varphi (t) ] \varphi^ {\prime} (t) \mathrm {d} t. \tag {4.2.3}
$$

式（4.2.3）右端的不定积分比较容易计算，再将求出的不定积分中的 $t$ 以 $t = \varphi^{-1}(x)$ 代入即可。在这里，为了保证反函数 $t = \varphi^{-1}(x)$ 及积分 $\int f[\varphi(t)]\varphi(t)\mathrm{d}t$ 的存在性，假定 $x = \varphi(t)$ 是单调的可微函数， $\varphi'(t) \neq 0$ ，且 $f[\varphi(t)]\varphi'(t)$ 具有原函数，这就是第二类换元法。

定理4.2.2 设

（1） $x = \varphi (t)$ 是单调的可微函数，且 $\varphi^{\prime}(t)\neq 0$ 

（2） $f[\varphi (t)]\varphi '(t)$ 具有原函数，

则

$$
\int f (x) \mathrm {d} x = \int f [ \varphi (t) ] \varphi^ {\prime} (t) \mathrm {d} t \Big | _ {t = \varphi^ {- 1} (x)}. \tag {4.2.4}
$$

证 设 $F(t)$ 是 $f[\varphi (t)]\varphi '(t)$ 的一个原函数，则

$$
\frac {\mathrm {d} F [ \varphi^ {- 1} (x) ]}{\mathrm {d} x} = F ^ {\prime} (t) [ \varphi^ {- 1} (x) ] ^ {\prime} = f [ \varphi (t) ] \varphi^ {\prime} (t) \frac {1}{\varphi^ {\prime} (t)} = f [ \varphi (t) ] = f (x).
$$

即 $F[\varphi^{-1}(x)]$ 是 $f(x)$ 的一个原函数，于是

$$
\int f (x) \mathrm {d} x = F [ \varphi^ {- 1} (x) ] + C = \int f [ \varphi (t) ] \varphi^ {\prime} (t) \mathrm {d} t \Big | _ {t = \varphi^ {- 1} (x)}.
$$

这样便证明了式（4.2.4）.

例4.2.16 求 $\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{1 + \sqrt{2x + 1}}$ 

解 计算此积分的主要困难在于被积函数中含有根式. 为了消去根式, 令 $\sqrt{2x + 1} = t$ , 即 $x = \frac{t^2 - 1}{2} (t > 0)$ , 则 $\mathrm{d}x = t\mathrm{d}t$ , 于是

$$
\begin{array}{l} \int \frac {\mathrm {d} x}{1 + \sqrt {2 x + 1}} = \int \frac {\mathrm {t d} t}{1 + t} = \int \left(1 - \frac {1}{1 + t}\right) \mathrm {d} t \\ = t - \ln | 1 + t | + C = \sqrt {2 x + 1} - \ln | 1 + \sqrt {2 x + 1} | + C. \\ \end{array}
$$

例4.2.17 求 $\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{x} + \sqrt[3]{x}}.$ 

解为了消去被积函数中的根式，令 $\sqrt[6]{x} = t$ ，即 $x = t^6 (t > 0)$ ，则

$$
\mathrm {d} x = 6 t ^ {5} \mathrm {d} t, \frac {1}{\sqrt {x} + \sqrt [ 3 ]{x}} = \frac {1}{t ^ {3} + t ^ {2}},
$$

于是

$$
\begin{array}{l} \int \frac {\mathrm {d} x}{\sqrt {x} + \sqrt [ 3 ]{x}} = \int \frac {6 t ^ {5}}{t ^ {3} + t ^ {2}} \mathrm {d} t = 6 \int \left(1 - t + t ^ {2} - \frac {1}{1 + t}\right) \mathrm {d} t \\ = 6 \left(t - \frac {t ^ {2}}{2} + \frac {t ^ {3}}{3} - \ln | 1 + t |\right) + C \\ = 6 \left[ \sqrt [ 6 ]{x} - \frac {\sqrt [ 3 ]{x}}{2} + \frac {\sqrt {x}}{3} - \ln (1 + \sqrt [ 6 ]{x}) \right] + C. \\ \end{array}
$$

例4.2.18 求 $\int \sqrt{a^2 - x^2}\mathrm{d}x(a > 0)$ 

解 利用三角恒等式 $\sin^2 t + \cos^2 t = 1$ 去掉被积函数中的根式. 不妨令

$$
x = a \sin t \left(- \frac {\pi}{2} <   t <   \frac {\pi}{2}\right),
$$

则 $\mathrm{dx} = a\cos t\mathrm{dt},\sqrt{a^2 - x^2} = a\cos t$ ，于是

$$
\begin{array}{l} \int \sqrt {a ^ {2} - x ^ {2}} d x = \int a ^ {2} \cos^ {2} t d t \\ = \frac {a ^ {2}}{2} \int (1 + \cos 2 t) d t \\ = \frac {a ^ {2}}{2} t + \frac {a ^ {2}}{4} \sin 2 t + C. \\ \end{array}
$$

根据变换 $x = a\sin t$ 作直角三角形（见图4.2.1），得

$$
\cos t = \frac {\sqrt {a ^ {2} - x ^ {2}}}{a},
$$

例4.2.19 求 $\int \frac{\sqrt{x^2 + a^2}}{x^2}\mathrm{d}x(a > 0).$ 

解 令 $x = a \tan t\left(-\frac{\pi}{2} < t < \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ，则 $\mathrm{dx} = a \sec^2 t \mathrm{dt}$ ，于是

$$
\begin{array}{l} \int \frac {\sqrt {x ^ {2} + a ^ {2}}}{x ^ {2}} d x = \int \frac {a \sec t}{a ^ {2} \tan^ {2} t} \cdot a \sec^ {2} t d t = \int \frac {1}{\sin^ {2} t \cdot \cos t} d t \\ = \int \frac {\sin^ {2} t + \cos^ {2} t}{\sin^ {2} t \cdot \cos t} d t \\ = \int \sec t d t + \int \cot t \cdot \csc t d t \\ = \ln | \sec t + \tan t | - \csc t + C _ {1}. \\ \end{array}
$$

由图4.2.2可知，

$$
\sec t = \frac {\sqrt {x ^ {2} + a ^ {2}}}{a}, \quad \tan t = \frac {x}{a}, \quad \csc t = \frac {\sqrt {x ^ {2} + a ^ {2}}}{x},
$$

因此，

$$
\begin{array}{l} \int \frac {\sqrt {x ^ {2} + a ^ {2}}}{x ^ {2}} d x = \ln \left| \frac {\sqrt {a ^ {2} + x ^ {2}}}{a} + \frac {x}{a} \right| - \frac {\sqrt {a ^ {2} + x ^ {2}}}{x} + C _ {1} \\ = \ln \left| x + \sqrt {a ^ {2} + x ^ {2}} \right| - \frac {\sqrt {a ^ {2} + x ^ {2}}}{x} - \ln a + C _ {1} \\ = \ln \left| x + \sqrt {a ^ {2} + x ^ {2}} \right| - \frac {\sqrt {a ^ {2} + x ^ {2}}}{x} + C. \\ \end{array}
$$

这里注意到，因为 $C_1$ 是任意常数，所以 $(- \ln a + C_1)$ 仍然是任意常数，那么可记为 $C$ 。以后出现类似情形，可以同样处理。

例4.2.20 求 $\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{x^2 - a^2}} (a > 0).$ 

解 当 $x > a$ 时，令 $x = a\sec t\left(0 < t < \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ，则

$$
\mathrm {d} x = a \sec t \cdot \tan t \mathrm {d} t, \sqrt {x ^ {2} - a ^ {2}} = a \tan t,
$$

于是

$$
\begin{array}{l} \int \frac {\mathrm {d} x}{\sqrt {x ^ {2} - a ^ {2}}} = \int \frac {a \sec t \cdot \tan t}{a \tan t} \mathrm {d} t \\ = \int \sec t d t = \ln (\sec t + \tan t) + C _ {1}. \\ \end{array}
$$

由图4.2.3可知，

$$
\sec t = \frac {x}{a}, \quad \tan t = \frac {\sqrt {x ^ {2} - a ^ {2}}}{a},
$$

![image](https://cdn-mineru.openxlab.org.cn/result/2026-04-03/ccf3acff-bbac-48d8-97dd-e31ada0e3759/823561686ddbf486f5b250c2ec6f74f088c4a3126f208d005e873fe14e68f348.jpg)

则

$$
\int \frac {\mathrm {d} x}{\sqrt {x ^ {2} - a ^ {2}}} = \ln \left(\frac {x}{a} + \frac {\sqrt {x ^ {2} - a ^ {2}}}{a}\right) + C _ {1} = \ln \left(x + \sqrt {x ^ {2} - a ^ {2}}\right) + C. \tag {4.2.5}
$$

当 $x < -a$ 时，令 $x = -u$ ，则 $u > a$ ，于是由式（4.2.5）可得

$$
\begin{array}{l} \int \frac {\mathrm {d} x}{\sqrt {x ^ {2} - a ^ {2}}} = - \int \frac {\mathrm {d} u}{\sqrt {u ^ {2} - a ^ {2}}} = - \ln \left(u + \sqrt {u ^ {2} - a ^ {2}}\right) + C _ {2} = \ln \frac {1}{u + \sqrt {u ^ {2} - a ^ {2}}} + C _ {2} \\ = \ln \frac {u - \sqrt {u ^ {2} - a ^ {2}}}{a ^ {2}} + C _ {2} = \ln \left(u - \sqrt {u ^ {2} - a ^ {2}}\right) + C = \ln \left(- x - \sqrt {x ^ {2} - a ^ {2}}\right) + C. \\ \end{array}
$$

综上可得，无论 $x > a$ 或 $x < -a$ ，都有

$$
\int \frac {\mathrm {d} x}{\sqrt {x ^ {2} - a ^ {2}}} = \ln \left| x + \sqrt {x ^ {2} - a ^ {2}} \right| + C.
$$

注 从例 $4.2.18 \sim 4.2.20$ 可知，若被积函数含有根式 $\sqrt{a^2 - x^2}, \sqrt{x^2 + a^2}, \sqrt{x^2 - a^2}$ ，则可以分别令 $x = a \sin t, x = a \tan t, x = a \sec t$ 消去根式，然后再计算积分。这些代换通常称为三角代换。需要强调的是，使用三角代换计算不定积分，最后进行变量还原时，一般采用辅助直角三角形进行。

例4.2.21 求 $\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x^2\sqrt{a^2 - x^2}} (a > 0).$ 

解 解法一 被积函数中含有根式 $\sqrt{a^2 - x^2}$ ，令 $x = a\sin t\left(-\frac{\pi}{2} < t < \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ，则

$$
\mathrm {d} x = a \cos t \mathrm {d} t,
$$

于是

$$
\int \frac {\mathrm {d} x}{x ^ {2} \sqrt {a ^ {2} - x ^ {2}}} = \int \frac {a \cos t \mathrm {d} t}{a ^ {2} \sin^ {2} t \cdot a \cos t} = \frac {1}{a ^ {2}} \int \csc^ {2} t \mathrm {d} t = - \frac {1}{a ^ {2}} \cot t + C = - \frac {\sqrt {a ^ {2} - x ^ {2}}}{a ^ {2} x} + C.
$$

解法二 令 $x = \frac{1}{t}$ （称之为倒代换），则 $\mathrm{dx} = -\frac{1}{t^2}\mathrm{dt}$ ，于是，当 $x > 0$ 时，

$$
\begin{array}{l} \int \frac {\mathrm {d} x}{x ^ {2} \sqrt {a ^ {2} - x ^ {2}}} = - \int \frac {t \mathrm {d} t}{\sqrt {a ^ {2} t ^ {2} - 1}} = - \frac {1}{2 a ^ {2}} \int (a ^ {2} t ^ {2} - 1) ^ {- \frac {1}{2}} \mathrm {d} (a ^ {2} t ^ {2} - 1) \\ = - \frac {1}{a ^ {2}} \sqrt {a ^ {2} t ^ {2} - 1} + C = - \frac {\sqrt {a ^ {2} - x ^ {2}}}{a ^ {2} x} + C. \\ \end{array}
$$

当 $x < 0$ 时，可得到相同结果

当被积函数是 $\frac{1}{x^2\sqrt{x^2 + a^2}}$ 或 $\frac{1}{x^2\sqrt{x^2 - a^2}}$ 时，也可作倒代换 $x = \frac{1}{t}$ 求其原函数

若被积函数含有二次根式 $\sqrt{ax^2 + bx + c}$ ( $a \neq 0$ ), 可以先将二次三项式 $ax^2 + bx + c$ 配方, 然后作三角代换.

例4.2.22 求 $\int \sqrt{-x^2 + 2x + 1}\mathrm{d}x.$ 

解 因为 $-x^{2} + 2x + 1 = 2 - (x - 1)^{2}$ ，所以令 $x - 1 = \sqrt{2}\sin t\left(-\frac{\pi}{2} < t < \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ，于是

$$
\begin{array}{l} \int \sqrt {- x ^ {2} + 2 x + 1} d x = \int \sqrt {2 - (x - 1) ^ {2}} d x = \int \sqrt {2} \cos t \cdot \sqrt {2} \cos t d t \\ = \int (1 + \cos 2 t) \mathrm {d} t = t + \frac {1}{2} \sin 2 t + C \\ = \arcsin \frac {x - 1}{\sqrt {2}} + \frac {x - 1}{2} \sqrt {- x ^ {2} + 2 x + 1} + C. \\ \end{array}
$$

例4.2.23 求 $\int \frac{2 - x}{\sqrt{2 + 2x + x^2}}\mathrm{d}x.$ 

解 因为 $2 + 2x + x^{2} = 1 + (x + 1)^{2}$ ，所以令 $x + 1 = \tan t\left(-\frac{\pi}{2} < t < \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ，于是

$$
\begin{array}{l} \int \frac {2 - x}{\sqrt {2 + 2 x + x ^ {2}}} d x = \int \frac {2 - x}{\sqrt {1 + (x + 1) ^ {2}}} d x \\ = \int \frac {3 - \tan t}{\sec t} \cdot \sec^ {2} t d t \\ = 3 \int \sec t d t - \int \tan t \sec t d t \\ = 3 \ln | \tan t + \sec t | - \sec t + C \\ = 3 \ln \left| x + 1 + \sqrt {2 + 2 x + x ^ {2}} \right| - \sqrt {2 + 2 x + x ^ {2}} + C. \\ \end{array}
$$

有时，在求不定积分时，两类换元法可交替使用

例4.2.24 求 $\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x + \sqrt{1 - x^2}}.$ 

解 令 $x = \sin t\left(-\frac{\pi}{2} < t < \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ 则

$$
\begin{array}{l} \int \frac {\mathrm {d} x}{x + \sqrt {1 - x ^ {2}}} = \int \frac {\cos t}{\sin t + \cos t} \mathrm {d} t = \frac {1}{2} \int \frac {2 \cos t}{\sin t + \cos t} \mathrm {d} t \\ = \frac {1}{2} \int \frac {(\cos t - \sin t) + (\sin t + \cos t)}{\sin t + \cos t} d t \\ = \frac {1}{2} \int \frac {1}{\sin t + \cos t} d (\sin t + \cos t) + \frac {1}{2} \int d t \\ = \frac {1}{2} \ln | \sin t + \cos t | + \frac {1}{2} t + C \\ = \frac {1}{2} \ln | x + \sqrt {1 - x ^ {2}} | + \frac {1}{2} \arcsin x + C. \\ \end{array}
$$

例4.2.25 求 $\int \frac{x^5}{\sqrt{1 + x^2}}\mathrm{d}x.$ 

解 令 $x = \tan t\left(-\frac{\pi}{2} < t < \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ 则

$$
\begin{array}{l} \int \frac {x ^ {5}}{\sqrt {1 + x ^ {2}}} d x = \int \frac {\tan^ {5} t}{\sec t} \cdot \sec^ {2} t d t = \int \tan^ {5} t \sec t d t = \int \tan^ {4} t d (\sec t) \\ = \int (\sec^ {2} t - 1) ^ {2} d (\sec t) = \int (\sec^ {4} t - 2 \sec^ {2} t + 1) d (\sec t) \\ = \frac {1}{5} \sec^ {5} t - \frac {2}{3} \sec^ {3} t + \sec t + C \\ = \frac {1}{5} \left(1 + x ^ {2}\right) ^ {\frac {5}{2}} - \frac {2}{3} \left(1 + x ^ {2}\right) ^ {\frac {3}{2}} + \left(1 + x ^ {2}\right) ^ {\frac {1}{2}} + C. \\ \end{array}
$$
