# 第三节 分部积分法

在第四章第二节中，我们在复合函数求导法则的基础上，得到了换元积分法。本节我们使用两个函数乘积的求导法则来推导分部积分法。

设函数 $u = u(x)$ 及 $\nu = \nu (x)$ 具有连续导数，将乘积的求导公式

$$
(u v) ^ {\prime} = u ^ {\prime} v + u v ^ {\prime}
$$

移项，可得

$$
u v ^ {\prime} = (u v) ^ {\prime} - u ^ {\prime} v, \tag {4.3.1}
$$

在式（4.3.1）两边求不定积分，得

$$
\int u v ^ {\prime} \mathrm {d} x = u v - \int u ^ {\prime} v \mathrm {d} x.
$$

这样，我们得到下面定理

定理4.3.1 设函数 $u = u(x)$ 及 $\nu = \nu (x)$ 具有连续导数，则

$$
\int u (x) v ^ {\prime} (x) \mathrm {d} x = u (x) v (x) - \int u ^ {\prime} (x) v (x) \mathrm {d} x,
$$

即

$$
\int u \mathrm {d} v = u v - \int v \mathrm {d} u. \tag {4.3.2}
$$

式（4.3.2）称为分部积分公式，运用式（4.3.2）求不定积分的方法称为分部积分法。若不定积分 $\int u\mathrm{d}\nu$ 不易求得，而 $\int \nu \mathrm{d}u$ 容易求得，则可以使用式（4.3.2）把求不定积分 $\int u\mathrm{d}\nu$ 的问题转化为求 $\int \nu \mathrm{d}u$ 。下面举例说明分部积分法的应用。

例4.3.1 求 $\int x\mathrm{e}^x\mathrm{d}x$ 

解 令 $u = x, \mathrm{d}\nu = \mathrm{e}^{x}\mathrm{d}x$ ，则 $\nu = \mathrm{e}^{x}, \mathrm{d}u = \mathrm{d}x$ ，由式（4.3.2）得

$$
\int x \mathrm {e} ^ {x} \mathrm {d} x = x \mathrm {e} ^ {x} - \int \mathrm {e} ^ {x} \mathrm {d} x = x \mathrm {e} ^ {x} - \mathrm {e} ^ {x} + C.
$$

注 若令 $u = \mathrm{e}^x$ $\mathrm{d}\nu = x\mathrm{d}x$ ，则 $\nu = \frac{1}{2} x^{2},\mathrm{d}u = \mathrm{e}^{x}\mathrm{d}x$ ，同样由式（4.3.1）得

$$
\int x \mathrm {e} ^ {x} \mathrm {d} x = \frac {x ^ {2}}{2} \mathrm {e} ^ {x} - \int \frac {x ^ {2}}{2} \mathrm {e} ^ {x} \mathrm {d} x. \tag {4.3.3}
$$

可见式（4.3.3）右端的第二项 $\int \frac{x^2}{2}\mathrm{e}^x\mathrm{d}x$ ，比所要求的不定积分 $\int x\mathrm{e}^{x}\mathrm{d}x$ 更复杂．这告诉我们，运用分部积分公式（4.3.2）计算不定积分时，适当选取 $u(x),v(x)$ 是关键，否则仍无法求出结果.

为方便，应用分部积分法时，常常将所求的不定积分先凑成 $\int u\mathrm{d}\nu$ 的形式，然后直接套用式（4.3.2）。例如：

$$
\int x \mathrm {e} ^ {2 x} \mathrm {d} x = \frac {1}{2} \int x \mathrm {d e} ^ {2 x} = \frac {1}{2} \left(x \mathrm {e} ^ {2 x} - \int \mathrm {e} ^ {2 x} \mathrm {d} x\right) = \frac {1}{2} \left(x \mathrm {e} ^ {2 x} - \frac {1}{2} \mathrm {e} ^ {2 x}\right) + C = \frac {2 x - 1}{4} \mathrm {e} ^ {2 x} + C.
$$

例4.3.2 求 $\int x^2\cos x\mathrm{d}x$ 

解 $\int x^{2}\cos x\mathrm{d}x = \int x^{2}\mathrm{d}(\sin x) = x^{2}\sin x - \int \sin x\mathrm{d}(x^{2})$ 

$$
\begin{array}{l} = \int x ^ {2} \mathrm {d} (\sin x) = x ^ {2} \sin x - \int \sin x \mathrm {d} (x ^ {2}) \\ = x ^ {2} \sin x - 2 \int x \sin x d x \\ = x ^ {2} \sin x + 2 \int x d (\cos x) \\ = x ^ {2} \sin x + 2 (x \cos x - \int \cos x d x) \\ = x ^ {2} \sin x + 2 (x \cos x - \sin x) + C. \\ \end{array}
$$

例4.3.2说明，分部积分法可多次应用

一般地，下述形式的不定积分均可应用分部积分法求解：

$$
\int x ^ {n} \mathrm {e} ^ {a x} \mathrm {d} x, \int x ^ {n} \sin (a x + b) \mathrm {d} x, \int x ^ {n} \cos (a x + b) \mathrm {d} x,
$$

其中 $n$ 为正整数， $a, b$ 为常数， $a \neq 0$ 。这时可分别令

$$
u = x ^ {n}, \mathrm {d} v = \mathrm {e} ^ {a x} \mathrm {d} x; u = x ^ {n}, \mathrm {d} v = \sin (a x + b) \mathrm {d} x; u = x ^ {n}, \mathrm {d} v = \cos (a x + b) \mathrm {d} x.
$$

例4.3.3 求 $\int x^{2}\ln (x - 1)\mathrm{d}x$ 

解 $\int x^{2}\ln (x - 1)\mathrm{d}x = \int \ln (x - 1)\mathrm{d}\left(\frac{x^{3}}{3}\right) = \frac{x^{3}}{3}\ln (x - 1) - \int \frac{x^{3}}{3}\mathrm{d}[\ln (x - 1)]$ 

$$
\begin{array}{l} = \frac {x ^ {3}}{3} \ln (x - 1) - \int \frac {x ^ {3}}{3} \cdot \frac {1}{x - 1} d x \\ = \frac {x ^ {3}}{3} \ln (x - 1) - \frac {1}{3} \int \left(x ^ {2} + x + 1 + \frac {1}{x - 1}\right) d x \\ = \frac {x ^ {3}}{3} \ln (x - 1) - \frac {1}{3} \left[ \frac {x ^ {3}}{3} + \frac {x ^ {2}}{2} + x + \ln (x - 1) \right] + C \\ = \frac {x ^ {3} - 1}{3} \ln (x - 1) - \frac {x ^ {3}}{9} - \frac {x ^ {2}}{6} - \frac {x}{3} + C. \\ \end{array}
$$

例4.3.4 求 $\int \arcsin x\mathrm{d}x$ 

解这个不定积分已经是 $\int u\mathrm{d}v$ 的形式，直接使用式（4.3.2）得

$$
\begin{array}{l} \int \arcsin x d x = x \arcsin x - \int x d (\arcsin x) = x \arcsin x - \int \frac {x}{\sqrt {1 - x ^ {2}}} d x \\ = x \arcsin x + \frac {1}{2} \int \frac {\mathrm {d} \left(1 - x ^ {2}\right)}{\sqrt {1 - x ^ {2}}} = x \arcsin x + \sqrt {1 - x ^ {2}} + C. \\ \end{array}
$$

例4.3.5 求 $\int x\arctan x\mathrm{d}x$ 

解 $\int x\arctan x\mathrm{d}x = \int \arctan x\mathrm{d}\left(\frac{x^2 + 1}{2}\right) = \frac{x^2 + 1}{2}\arctan x - \int \frac{x^2 + 1}{2}\mathrm{d}(\arctan x)$ 

$$
= \frac {x ^ {2} + 1}{2} \arctan x - \frac {1}{2} \int d x = \frac {x ^ {2} + 1}{2} \arctan x - \frac {x}{2} + C.
$$

一般地，形如

$$
\int x ^ {n} \arcsin x d x, \int x ^ {n} \arccos  x d x, \int x ^ {n} \arctan x d x, \int x ^ {n} \operatorname {a r c c o t} x d x, \int x ^ {n} \ln (a x + b) d x
$$

的不定积分（ $n$ 为正整数， $a, b$ 为常数， $a \neq 0$ ）均可应用分部积分法求解，此时应分别令

$$
u = \arcsin x, u = \arccos  x, u = \arctan x, u = \operatorname {a r c c o t} x, u = \ln (a x + b),
$$

而令 $\mathrm{d}\nu = x^n\mathrm{d}x$ 

例4.3.6 求 $\int \mathrm{e}^x\cos x\mathrm{d}x$ 

解 $\int \mathrm{e}^{x}\cos x\mathrm{d}x = \int \mathrm{e}^{x}\mathrm{d}(\sin x) = \mathrm{e}^{x}\sin x - \int \mathrm{e}^{x}\sin x\mathrm{d}x$ 

$$
= \mathrm {e} ^ {x} \sin x + \int \mathrm {e} ^ {x} \mathrm {d} (\cos x) = \mathrm {e} ^ {x} \sin x + \mathrm {e} ^ {x} \cos x - \int \mathrm {e} ^ {x} \cos x \mathrm {d} x.
$$

经过两次分部积分后，又出现了所求的不定积分 $\int \mathrm{e}^{x}\cos x\mathrm{d}x$ ，将其看作未知量，得到一个方程，移项得（此时等式右端已不含积分项，所以必须加任意常数 $C_1$ ）

$$
2 \int \mathrm {e} ^ {x} \cos x \mathrm {d} x = \mathrm {e} ^ {x} \sin x + \mathrm {e} ^ {x} \cos x + C _ {1}. \tag {4.3.4}
$$

式（4.3.4）两边同除以2，得

$$
\int \mathrm {e} ^ {x} \cos x \mathrm {d} x = \frac {1}{2} \mathrm {e} ^ {x} (\sin x + \cos x) + C.
$$

例4.3.7 求 $\int \sec^3 x\mathrm{d}x$ 

解 $\int \sec^3 x\mathrm{d}x = \int \sec x\mathrm{d}(\tan x) = \sec x\cdot \tan x - \int \tan x\mathrm{d}(\sec x)$ 

$$
\begin{array}{l} = \sec x \cdot \tan x - \int \sec x \cdot \tan^ {2} x d x \\ = \sec x \cdot \tan x - \int \sec x (\sec^ {2} x - 1) d x \\ = \sec x \cdot \tan x - \int \sec^ {3} x d x + \int \sec x d x \\ = \sec x \cdot \tan x - \int \sec^ {3} x d x + \ln | \sec x + \tan x |, \\ \end{array}
$$

则

$$
\int \sec^ {3} x d x = \frac {1}{2} \sec x \cdot \tan x + \frac {1}{2} \ln | \sec x + \tan x | + C.
$$

同理可得

$$
\int \csc^ {3} x d x = - \frac {1}{2} \csc x \cdot \cot x + \frac {1}{2} \ln | \csc x - \cot x | + C.
$$

例4.3.8 设 $f(x)$ 的原函数为 $\frac{\sin x}{x}$ ，求 $\int xf'(x) \, \mathrm{d}x$ .

解 由于

$$
\int f (x) \mathrm {d} x = \frac {\sin x}{x} + C,
$$

则

$$
f (x) = \frac {x \cos x - \sin x}{x ^ {2}},
$$

于是

$$
\int x f ^ {\prime} (x) \mathrm {d} x = \int x \mathrm {d} [ f (x) ] = x f (x) - \int f (x) \mathrm {d} x
$$

$$
\begin{array}{l} = x \cdot \frac {x \cos x - \sin x}{x ^ {2}} - \frac {\sin x}{x} + C \\ = \cos x - \frac {2 \sin x}{x} + C. \\ \end{array}
$$

使用分部积分公式可以推导一些递推公式

例4.3.9 求 $I_{n} = \int \sin^{n} x \, \mathrm{d}x$ （ $n$ 为正整数）：

解 当 $n = 1$ 时， $I_{1} = \int \sin x \, \mathrm{d}x = -\cos x + C.$ 

当 $n = 2$ 时，

$$
I _ {2} = \int \sin^ {2} x \mathrm {d} x = \frac {1}{2} \int (1 - \cos 2 x) \mathrm {d} x = \frac {x}{2} - \frac {1}{4} \sin 2 x + C.
$$

当 $n\geqslant 3$ 时，

$$
\begin{array}{l} I _ {n} = \int \sin^ {n} x d x = \int \sin^ {n - 1} x d (- \cos x) \\ = - \sin^ {n - 1} x \cdot \cos x + \int \cos x d (\sin^ {n - 1} x) \\ = - \sin^ {n - 1} x \cdot \cos x + (n - 1) \int \sin^ {n - 2} x \cdot \cos^ {2} x d x \\ = - \sin^ {n - 1} x \cdot \cos x + (n - 1) \int \sin^ {n - 2} x \left(1 - \sin^ {2} x\right) d x \\ = - \sin^ {n - 1} x \cdot \cos x + (n - 1) \int \sin^ {n - 2} x d x - (n - 1) \int \sin^ {n} x d x. \\ \end{array}
$$

从而

$$
I _ {n} = - \frac {1}{n} \sin^ {n - 1} x \cdot \cos x + \frac {n - 1}{n} I _ {n - 2} (n \geqslant 3). \tag {4.3.5}
$$

递推公式（4.3.5）将正弦函数高次幂的积分化为低次幂的积分（降两次），通过连续运用，可代为不定积分 $\int \sin x\mathrm{d}x$ 或 $\int \sin^2 x\mathrm{d}x$ ，从而可求得 $I_{n}$ $(n\geqslant 3)$ 

例如，由递推公式（4.3.5）可得

$$
I _ {6} = - \frac {1}{6} \sin^ {5} x \cdot \cos x + \frac {5}{6} \int \sin^ {4} x d x,
$$

而

$$
\begin{array}{l} I _ {4} = \int \sin^ {4} x d x = - \frac {1}{4} \sin^ {3} x \cdot \cos x + \frac {3}{4} \int \sin^ {2} x d x \\ = - \frac {1}{4} \sin^ {3} x \cdot \cos x + \frac {3}{4} \left(\frac {x}{2} - \frac {1}{4} \sin 2 x\right) + C, \\ \end{array}
$$

于是

$$
I _ {6} = \int \sin^ {6} x \mathrm {d} x = - \frac {1}{6} \sin^ {5} x \cdot \cos x - \frac {5}{2 4} \sin^ {3} x \cdot \cos x + \frac {5}{1 6} x - \frac {5}{3 2} \sin 2 x + C.
$$

同理可得如下递推公式：

$$
\int \cos^ {n} x d x = \frac {1}{n} \cos^ {n - 1} x \cdot \sin x + \frac {n - 1}{n} \int \cos^ {n - 2} x d x (n \geqslant 3).
$$

例4.3.10 求 $J_{n} = \int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{(x^{2} + a^{2})^{n}}$ （ $n$ 为正整数， $a > 0$ ）：

解 当 $n = 1$ 时，

$$
J _ {1} = \int \frac {\mathrm {d} x}{x ^ {2} + a ^ {2}} = \frac {1}{a} \arctan \frac {x}{a} + C.
$$

当 $n\geqslant 2$ 时，因为

$$
\begin{array}{l} J _ {n - 1} = \int \frac {\mathrm {d} x}{\left(x ^ {2} + a ^ {2}\right) ^ {n - 1}} = \frac {x}{\left(x ^ {2} + a ^ {2}\right) ^ {n - 1}} - \int x \mathrm {d} \left[ \frac {1}{\left(x ^ {2} + a ^ {2}\right) ^ {n - 1}} \right] \\ = \frac {x}{\left(x ^ {2} + a ^ {2}\right) ^ {n - 1}} + 2 (n - 1) \int \frac {x ^ {2}}{\left(x ^ {2} + a ^ {2}\right) ^ {n}} d x \\ = \frac {x}{\left(x ^ {2} + a ^ {2}\right) ^ {n - 1}} + 2 (n - 1) \int \frac {x ^ {2} + a ^ {2} - a ^ {2}}{\left(x ^ {2} + a ^ {2}\right) ^ {n}} d x \\ = \frac {x}{\left(x ^ {2} + a ^ {2}\right) ^ {n - 1}} + 2 (n - 1) J _ {n - 1} - 2 (n - 1) a ^ {2} J _ {n}, \\ \end{array}
$$

所以有递推公式

$$
J _ {n} = \frac {1}{(2 n - 2) a ^ {2}} \left[ \frac {x}{\left(x ^ {2} + a ^ {2}\right) ^ {n - 1}} + (2 n - 3) J _ {n - 1} \right] (n \geqslant 2).
$$

从而，由 $J_{1} = \frac{1}{a}\arctan \frac{x}{a} +C$ 可求得 $J_{n}(n\geqslant 2)$ 

例如，

$$
J _ {2} = \int \frac {\mathrm {d} x}{\left(x ^ {2} + a ^ {2}\right) ^ {2}} = \frac {1}{2 a ^ {2}} \left(\frac {x}{x ^ {2} + a ^ {2}} + J _ {1}\right) = \frac {1}{2 a ^ {2}} \left(\frac {x}{x ^ {2} + a ^ {2}} + \frac {1}{a} \arctan \frac {x}{a}\right) + C.
$$

下面举几个综合利用换元积分法和分部积分法的例子

例4.3.11 求 $\int \sin (\sqrt{x} + 1) \, \mathrm{d}x$ .

解 令 $\sqrt{x} = t$ ，则 $x = t^2, \mathrm{d}x = 2t\mathrm{d}t$ ，于是

$$
\begin{array}{l} \int \sin (\sqrt {x} + 1) d x = 2 \int t \sin (t + 1) d t = - 2 \int t d [ \cos (t + 1) ] \\ = - 2 [ t \cos (t + 1) - \int \cos (t + 1) \mathrm {d} t ] \\ = - 2 [ t \cos (t + 1) - \sin (t + 1) ] + C \\ = - 2 \sqrt {x} \cos (\sqrt {x} + 1) + 2 \sin (\sqrt {x} + 1) + C. \\ \end{array}
$$

例4.3.12 求 $\int \frac{x\arctan x}{\sqrt{(1 + x^2)^3}}\mathrm{d}x.$ 

解 令 $x = \tan t$ ，则

$$
\begin{array}{l} \int \frac {x \arctan x}{\sqrt {\left(1 + x ^ {2}\right) ^ {3}}} \mathrm {d} x = \int \frac {\tan t \cdot t}{\sec^ {3} t} \cdot \sec^ {2} t \mathrm {d} t = \int t \sin t \mathrm {d} t \\ = - \int t d (\cos t) = - t \cos t + \int \cos t d t \\ = - t \cos t - \sin t + C \\ = - \frac {1}{\sqrt {1 + x ^ {2}}} \arctan x - \frac {x}{\sqrt {1 + x ^ {2}}} + C. \\ \end{array}
$$

例4.3.13 设 $F(x)$ 为 $f(x)$ 的一个原函数， $F'(x)$ 连续， $F(0) = 1$ ， $F(x) > 0$ ，且当 $x \geqslant 0$ 时，有

$$
f (x) F (x) = \frac {x \mathrm {e} ^ {x}}{2 (1 + x) ^ {2}},
$$

求 $f(x)$ 

解 由 $f(x) = F'(x)$ ，将其代入题设中方程得

$$
2 F (x) F ^ {\prime} (x) = \frac {x \mathrm {e} ^ {x}}{(1 + x) ^ {2}},
$$

于是

$$
\int 2 F (x) F ^ {\prime} (x) \mathrm {d} x = \int \frac {x \mathrm {e} ^ {x}}{(1 + x) ^ {2}} \mathrm {d} x,
$$

从而

$$
\begin{array}{l} F ^ {2} (x) = - \int x \mathrm {e} ^ {x} \mathrm {d} \left(\frac {1}{1 + x}\right) = - \frac {x \mathrm {e} ^ {x}}{x + 1} + \int \frac {1}{x + 1} \mathrm {d} \left(x \mathrm {e} ^ {x}\right) \\ = - \frac {x \mathrm {e} ^ {x}}{x + 1} + \int \frac {x + 1}{x + 1} \mathrm {e} ^ {x} \mathrm {d} x = - \frac {x \mathrm {e} ^ {x}}{x + 1} + \mathrm {e} ^ {x} + C = \frac {\mathrm {e} ^ {x}}{1 + x} + C. \\ \end{array}
$$

由 $F(0) = 1$ 及 $F^2 (0) = 1 + C$ ，得 $C = 0$ ，因为 $F(x) > 0$ ，所以

$$
F (x) = \sqrt {\frac {\mathrm {e} ^ {x}}{1 + x}},
$$

从而

$$
f (x) = \frac {x \mathrm {e} ^ {\frac {x}{2}}}{2 (1 + x) ^ {\frac {3}{2}}}.
$$
