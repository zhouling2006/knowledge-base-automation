### 习题5.4

1. 判别下列反常积分的敛散性，若收敛，计算其值：

(1) $\int_0^{+\infty}\frac{x}{(1 + x)^3}\mathrm{d}x;$

(2) $\int_{\mathrm{c}}^{+\infty}\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x\ln^2x};$

(3) $\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{2x}{1 + x^2}\mathrm{d}x;$

(4) $\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x^2 + 2x + 2};$

(5) $\int_0^{+\infty}\mathrm{e}^{-x}\sin x\mathrm{d}x$

(6) $\int_0^1\frac{x\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}};$

(7) $\int_{a}^{2a}\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{(x - a)^{\frac{3}{2}}} (a > 0)$

(8) $\int_{-\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}} \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\cos^2 x}$ ;

(9) $\int_0^2\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x^2 - 4x + 3}$

(10) $\int_0^{+\infty}\mathrm{e}^{-\sqrt{x}}\mathrm{d}x$

(11) $\int_{1}^{+\infty}\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x\sqrt{x^2 - 1}};$

(12) $\int_0^1\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{x(1 - x)}}.$

2. 已知 $\lim_{x\to \infty}\left(\frac{1 + x}{x}\right)^{\alpha x} = \int_{-\infty}^{\alpha}t\mathrm{e}^{t}\mathrm{d}t$ ，求常数 $\alpha$
