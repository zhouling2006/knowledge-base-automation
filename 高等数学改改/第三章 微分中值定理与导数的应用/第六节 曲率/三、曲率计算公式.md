### 三、曲率计算公式

设曲线 $C$ 的直角坐标方程为 $y = f(x)$ ，且 $f(x)$ 具有二阶导数，那么 $f^{\prime}(x)$ 连续，从而曲线 $C$ 是光滑的．记曲线切线的倾角为 $\alpha$ ，由导数的几何意义可知 $\tan \alpha = y'$ ，两边对 $x$ 求导有

$$
\sec^ {2} \alpha \cdot \frac {\mathrm {d} \alpha}{\mathrm {d} x} = y ^ {\prime \prime},
$$

则

$$
\frac {\mathrm {d} \alpha}{\mathrm {d} x} = \frac {y ^ {\prime \prime}}{\sec^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {y ^ {\prime \prime}}{1 + \tan^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {y ^ {\prime \prime}}{1 + y ^ {\prime 2}},
$$

于是

$$
\mathrm {d} \alpha = \frac {y ^ {\prime \prime}}{1 + y ^ {\prime 2}} \mathrm {d} x.
$$

又因为由式（3.6.2）可知 $\mathrm{ds} = \sqrt{1 + y'^2}\mathrm{dx}$ ，所以

$$
K = \left| \frac {\mathrm {d} \alpha}{\mathrm {d} s} \right| = \frac {| y ^ {\prime \prime} |}{\left(1 + y ^ {\prime 2}\right) ^ {\frac {3}{2}}}. \tag {3.6.3}
$$

设光滑曲线 $C$ 由参数方程

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = \varphi (t), \\ y = \psi (t) \end{array} \right.
$$

给出，其中 $\varphi(t), \psi(t)$ 具有二阶导数，且 $\varphi'^2(t) + \psi'^2(t) \neq 0$ ，则根据参数方程所确定函数的求导法则及式（3.6.3）可得

$$
K = \frac {\left| \varphi^ {\prime} (t) \psi^ {\prime \prime} (t) - \psi^ {\prime} (t) \varphi^ {\prime \prime} (t) \right|}{\left[ \varphi^ {\prime 2} (t) + \psi^ {\prime 2} (t) \right] ^ {\frac {3}{2}}}. \tag {3.6.4}
$$

例3.6.2 计算曲线 $y = \cos x$ 在点 $M(0,1)$ 处的曲率

解因为 $y^\prime = -\sin x,y^{\prime \prime} = -\cos x$ ，所以该曲线在任意点处的曲率为

$$
K = \frac {\left| - \cos x \right|}{\left(1 + \sin^ {2} x\right) ^ {\frac {3}{2}}}
$$

在点 $M(0,1)$ 处有

$$
K \big | _ {x = 0} = \frac {\left| - \cos x \right|}{\left(1 + \sin^ {2} x\right) ^ {\frac {3}{2}}} \Bigg | _ {x = 0} = 1.
$$

例3.6.3 求摆线 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}x = a(t - \sin t),\\ y = a(1 - \cos t) \end{array} \right.$ （ $a > 0$ )在点 $M(\pi a,2a)$ 处的曲率

解 因为

$$
\frac {\mathrm {d} y}{\mathrm {d} x} = \frac {\frac {\mathrm {d} y}{\mathrm {d} t}}{\frac {\mathrm {d} x}{\mathrm {d} t}} = \frac {a \sin t}{a (1 - \cos t)}, \quad \frac {\mathrm {d} ^ {2} y}{\mathrm {d} x ^ {2}} = \frac {\frac {\mathrm {d}}{\mathrm {d} t} \left(\frac {\mathrm {d} y}{\mathrm {d} x}\right)}{\frac {\mathrm {d} x}{\mathrm {d} t}} = \frac {- 1}{a (1 - \cos t) ^ {2}},
$$

在点 $M(\pi a,2a)$ 处对应的参数 $t = \pi$ ，所以

$$
y ^ {\prime} (\pi) = \frac {\mathrm {d} y}{\mathrm {d} x} \bigg | _ {t = \pi} = 0, \quad y ^ {\prime \prime} (\pi) = \frac {\mathrm {d} ^ {2} y}{\mathrm {d} x ^ {2}} \bigg | _ {t = \pi} = - \frac {1}{4 a}.
$$

于是摆线在点 $M$ 处的曲率为

$$
K \Big | _ {t = \pi} = \frac {\mid y ^ {\prime \prime} (\pi) \mid}{\left[ 1 + [ y ^ {\prime} (\pi) ] ^ {2} \right] ^ {\frac {3}{2}}} = \frac {1}{4 a}.
$$

注 例3.6.3也可以直接使用式(3.6.4)计算
